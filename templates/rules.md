# 📜 SERVER RULES & COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Welcome to our gaming community! To ensure everyone has a fun, safe, and competitive environment, please read and follow our server rules below.

---

### 🟢 1. Respect & General Conduct
- **Treat everyone with respect**: No toxicity, hate speech, racism, sexism, homophobia, or harassment of any kind.
- **Keep it friendly**: Friendly banter and competitive talk during games are fine, but keep it healthy and non-toxic.
- **No Drama**: Keep personal conflicts out of public text and voice channels. Take it to private DMs or contact a moderator.

---

### 🎮 2. Voice Channels & Gaming Etiquette
- **No Mic Spamming**: Avoid loud noises, ear-rape audio, constant throat clearing, or heavy background noise. Use **Push to Talk** or adjust your noise suppression if needed.
- **Respect Squad Limits**: Do not join full game squad VCs unless invited or there is an open slot.
- **No Ghosting or Stream Sniping**: Sharing team positions or stream sniping fellow server members is strictly prohibited.
- **Play Fair**: Zero tolerance for cheating, hacking, exploit abuse, or third-party unauthorized software in any multiplayer game (Valorant, BGMI, Rocket League, CS2, etc.).

---

### 💬 3. Text Channels & Content Guidelines
- **Use Channels Correctly**:
  - Keep game-specific talk in their respective channels (`#valorant`, `#rocket-league`, `#bgmi`).
  - Share memes only in `#meme-central`.
  - Share game clips and highlights in `#media-and-clips`.
  - Use `#bot-commands` for bot commands.
- **No Spamming**: Avoid flood messages, excessive capital letters, wall-of-text, or tagging `@everyone` / `@here` unnecessarily.
- **NSFW Content**: Absolutely NO NSFW, explicit, gore, or illegal content anywhere on the server.
- **No Self-Promotion or Advertising**: Do not advertise your Twitch/YouTube streams, other Discord servers, or commercial products without explicit staff approval.

---

### 🛡️ 4. Moderation & Staff Authority
- **Staff Instructions**: Follow the directions given by **👑 Admins** and **🛡️ Moderators**.
- **Reporting Issues**: If someone is breaking rules or causing trouble, ping a `@Moderator` or open a ticket.
- **Rule Enforcement**: Breaking rules may result in warnings, temporary mutes, kicks, or permanent bans depending on severity.

---

*Have fun and happy gaming! 🚀*
