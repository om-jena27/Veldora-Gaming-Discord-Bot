### 📌 How to Get Started:
1️⃣ Read our server guidelines in 📜 **#rules-and-info**.
2️⃣ Grab your game roles in 🏷️ **#get-roles** to get notified for squad matches.

4️⃣ Want to play right now? Use `/lfg` in 👥 **#looking-for-group** or jump into a squad Voice Channel!

Let's build an awesome community together. Invite your friends and let's game! 🚀🎮
