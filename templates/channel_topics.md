# 📌 DISCORD CHANNEL TOPIC DESCRIPTIONS

Copy and paste these topic descriptions into your Discord channel settings (`Edit Channel -> Channel Topic`).

---

### 📋 INFORMATION & WELCOME
- **`#rules-and-info`**: 📜 Official server rules, guidelines, and community information. Please read before chatting!
- **`#get-roles`**: 🏷️ Click the buttons below to assign yourself game roles (Valorant, Rocket League, BGMI) & interests!
- **`#welcome-and-goodbye`**: 👋 Welcome new members! Say hi and introduce yourself!

### 💬 COMMUNITY CHAT
- **`#general-chat`**: 💬 Main hangout text channel for general discussions & chatting with friends.
- **`#announcements`**: 📢 Important server updates, event announcements, and news.
- **`#bot-commands`**: 🤖 Use bot commands here to keep other text channels clean!

### 🎮 GAMING LOUNGE
- **`#gaming-discussion`**: 🎮 Talk about gaming news, upcoming titles, updates, and game reviews.
- **`#valorant`**: 🎯 Valorant highlights, rank updates, strat discussions, and agent setups.
- **`#rocket-league`**: 🚗 Rocket League trades, freestyle clips, rank flex, and car designs.
- **`#bgmi`**: 📱 BGMI / PUBG Mobile room codes, chicken dinners, and squad setups.
- **`#looking-for-group`**: 👥 Find squadmates! Use `/lfg` command to ping game roles and recruit players.

### 🎉 FUN & MEDIA
- **`#meme-central`**: 🤡 Share memes, jokes, and funny gaming clips. Auto-memes posted daily!
- **`#media-and-clips`**: 📷 Share your gaming clips, screenshots, setups, and fan art.
- **`#suggestions`**: 💡 Share your ideas and recommendations to improve the server.

### 🎵 MUSIC ZONE
- **`#music-chat`**: 🎶 Queue songs and manage music bot commands for `🎧 Music Vibes VC`.
